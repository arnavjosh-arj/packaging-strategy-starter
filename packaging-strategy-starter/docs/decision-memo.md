# Packaging Decision Memo (draft for discussion)

**Status:** teaching draft. Built only from the four source documents used in the workshop series.

## Question

Should we switch to eco-friendly, compostable packaging for our flagship product line?

## Key facts from the source documents

- **Market** (01_market_research_report): $9.8B market growing 8.5%. The Northeast and West account for 62% of demand.
- **Competitors** (02_competitor_analysis_memo): GreenWrap has 15% share and charges a 25-30% premium, strongest in the Northeast. EcoCase has 11% share and a 15-18% premium, strongest in the Midwest and West. Terra has 8% share, mainly with small and mid-sized retailers.
- **Customers** (03_customer_feedback_summary): 68% will pay up to a 15% premium. Only 22% will pay above 20%. The Northeast is the most tolerant (19% on average) and the Midwest the least (9% on average).
- **Policy** (04_sustainability_policy_brief): premiums above 15% require a business case memo and finance committee review. Certification must be third-party, never self-reported.

## Draft recommendation (for discussion)

Proceed with compostable packaging, starting in the regions where customers tolerate a premium best (the Northeast and West). Keep supplier premiums at or below 15% where possible. Any quote above 15% goes through a business case memo and finance committee review, and any certification claim must be confirmed as third-party.

*This recommendation is an inference from the facts above, not a finding stated in the documents.*

## Open questions

- Which supplier quotes are above the 15% line, and what is the business case for each?
- Which certifications have been verified by a third party?
- How should the premium be split between price and margin?
